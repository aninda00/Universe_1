After the great war of chaos, survival had become the only religion to follow. When humans realized that squabble amongst every species across the void only bred destruction, a peace treaty was forged, meticulously removing the history of the old world from the minds of people. Soon after, people started to worship the peace they had bled for centuries for, a peace that will endure for the next few millennia or so. But time the greatest destructor of everything beautiful and evil, slowly chipped away at the cherished peace.
