# THE PEACE 
After the great war of chaos, survival had become the only instinct to follow. When humans realized that discord amongst every species across the void only bred destruction, a peace treaty was forged, meticulously removing the history of the old world from the minds of people. Soon after, people started to worship the peace they had bled for centuries, a peace that will endure for the next few millennia or so. But time, the greatest destructor of everything beautiful and evil, slowly chipped away at their cherished peace.
